---
Read `docs/design.md` and the plan. Choose a mode from the arguments (default: hold): expansion (find the 10x version), selective (expand only where leverage is high), hold (keep scope, harden it), reduction (cut to the smallest valuable core).
Work through: the real user outcome, what is missing that a delighted user would expect, what can be deleted, sequencing risks, and what is deliberately out of scope.
Amend the plan accordingly and record the decision and reasons. End with `VERDICT: APPROVE` or `VERDICT: REJECT: <reasons>`.
